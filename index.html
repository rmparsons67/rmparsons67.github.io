Made by students who wish they had this tool in their graduating year.

An essential tool for getting prepared for every exam this year!

  • 10 years worth of HSC questions

    Uses 10 years of prior HSC questions to create a catalogue to ask only the most relevant questions for the current years syllabus.

  • Create a relevant exam for any point of the year

    All the questions are separated into relevant syllabus points allowing the student to create exams for only the parts they have learnt.

  • Multiple Versions of almost every question

    Multiple versions of the same question reinforces the student to learn the method to the answer rather than the specific question.
